Frequently Asked Questions

What is ScopeProof?
ScopeProof is a standalone iOS app for contractors who need to document and get paid for scope changes on the job. Build a change order with itemized line items, capture a customer signature on the spot or remotely, and generate a professional PDF — all in under a minute, offline, with no subscription.
How do I create a change order?
Tap the + button on the home screen. ScopeProof auto-fills a smart job name from your current location (street + date) to save typing. Add line items (description, quantity, unit price, unit), optionally attach photos as evidence, and tag customer details. Totals calculate automatically using your currency.
How does signature capture work?
When the job is done, hand your device to the customer. They review the change order and sign directly on the screen with a finger or Apple Pencil. The signature is saved as a crisp PNG and sealed with the order — together with the timestamp and, if enabled, GPS and device info.
What is remote signing?
Remote signing (Pro) lets customers sign a change order from anywhere. ScopeProof sends them a secure web link via email, SMS, or clipboard. They open it in their browser, review the order, and sign. The signature comes back to your app automatically via push notification. Change order data is encrypted end-to-end — the decryption key lives only in the URL you send, never on our servers.
How is remote signing kept private and secure?
When you send a change order for remote signing, it's encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before anything leaves the device. Only the encrypted payload is uploaded to our backend (Firebase).
  • The decryption key is never uploaded. It's embedded in the URL fragment (the part after #), which browsers never send to servers — so only you and your customer ever have it.
  • Signing requests automatically expire after 7 days. After that, the link stops working.
  • ScopeProof signs into Firebase anonymously — no account, no email, no identifier about you is sent.
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) are used only to alert you when the customer views, signs, rejects, or lets the request expire.
Full details are in our privacy policy.
What is Dispute-Proof mode?
Dispute-Proof mode (Pro) cryptographically seals a signed change order with SHA-256 hashing and an HMAC-SHA256 signature generated with a device-local key. The hash covers line items, attached photos, the customer signature, GPS, and the signing timestamp. If anyone alters any of it later, verification will detect the tampering. You can re-verify any signed order at any time from the detail view.
What is the evidence package?
The evidence package (Pro) is a ZIP file you can export for any signed change order. It contains the original photos, the customer signature image, the signed PDF, a machine-readable proof manifest (SHA-256 hashes, GPS, timestamps), and a README explaining how anyone can independently verify the hashes using standard tools like shasum. Useful for legal disputes, insurance claims, or simply your own records.
What are line item templates?
Templates (Pro) let you save frequently used line items — with a description, unit, default price, and category (Plumbing, Electrical, etc.) — and reuse them across change orders. You can even attach a price multiplier for customer-specific markups. Templates track usage so your most-used items surface first.
What are AI line item suggestions?
Attach a photo to a change order and ScopeProof's on-device AI (using Apple's Vision framework) reads any visible text and suggests relevant line items across trades — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, carpentry, flooring, painting, roofing, and more. It works entirely on your device, with no cloud calls. Available on both Free and Pro tiers.
What's included in Free vs Pro?
Free includes up to 5 change orders at a time, 5 line items per order, 1 photo per order, up to 2 projects, in-person signature capture, and watermarked PDFs. Pro ($9.99 once) unlocks: unlimited change orders, remote signing (email / SMS / link), unlimited line items and photos, unlimited projects, Dispute-Proof mode, evidence ZIP export, line item templates, company logo + brand color + contractor signature on PDFs, watermark removal, and QuickBooks IIF + JSON exports. One purchase, forever — no subscription.
Can I add my company branding?
Yes (Pro). Upload your company logo, set a brand color, and add your contractor signature. Your branding appears on every PDF — header, accent lines, and the signature block at the bottom.
What export formats are available?
All tiers can export professional PDFs (US Letter, multi-page) and CSV summaries. Pro adds QuickBooks IIF files for accounting import, JSON exports of full change order history, and the evidence ZIP for Dispute-Proof orders. Share via email, Messages, AirDrop, or save to Files.
Can I edit a change order after it's signed?
No — signed change orders are intentionally immutable to preserve legal integrity. If something needs to change, use Duplicate as New Draft (starts fresh) or Create Revision (links to the original via a revision chain so the history stays clear). Both options are available from the detail view of a signed order.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Change order creation, line items, photos, signature capture, PDF generation, CSV export, and integrity verification all work without a network connection. The only online features are remote signing (which requires a network to deliver the secure link and receive the returned signature) and App Store purchase validation.
Does ScopeProof work with TradeSnap?
ScopeProof and TradeSnap are companion apps from the same developer. ScopeProof handles signed change orders with line items and pricing. TradeSnap handles before/after photo documentation. They share design language but not data — each is standalone. Use both if your workflow needs both, or either one on its own.
Is my data private?
Yes. All change order data stays on your device by default. There are no analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks, and no account is required. The only exception is remote signing (Pro): when you use it, the change order is uploaded in encrypted form to Firebase so the customer's web browser can fetch it — and the decryption key stays in the URL fragment you send them, so it never reaches our servers. See our privacy policy for full details.
